# User guide

## Customers

**Create an account.** Choose Register, enter your name, email, phone (optional) and a password with at least 8 characters, including a letter and a number.

**Book a service.**
1. Open Services, search or filter by category, and open a service.
2. Choose Book this service (log in first if asked).
3. Pick a date, then an available time slot. Slots marked Full or Not available cannot be chosen.
4. Choose a saved address or enter a new one. Add notes for the technician if useful.
5. Choose Cash on service or Pay online, then Confirm booking.

**What happens next.** Your booking starts as Pending. Our team confirms it, then assigns a technician who accepts the job. You get an email at each step, and My bookings shows the progress.

**Paying online.** For fixed-price services the Pay now button appears on the booking page straight away. For estimate-priced services it appears after the technician completes the job and sets the final amount.

**Cancelling.** Open the booking and choose Cancel booking. Cancelling more than the stated number of hours before your slot is free. After that a percentage fee applies, and the page shows the policy before you confirm. Paid online bookings are refunded, minus any fee, after the team processes the refund.

**Reviews.** After a job is completed, open Reviews, choose Write a review, give 1 to 5 stars and an optional comment. You can review each booking once.

**Your account.** Profile and addresses lets you update your details, save addresses and change your password. Changing the password signs you out everywhere.

## Technicians

1. **Log in** with the account the administrator created for you. Change the temporary password under Profile.
2. **Overview** shows how many jobs wait for your answer, your active jobs and this month's earnings.
3. **Open a job** to see the customer's name, phone, address, notes and time slot.
4. **Respond:** Accept job, or Reject job with an optional reason. A rejected job goes back to the administrator.
5. **Work the job:** Start job when you arrive, then Complete job when you finish. For estimate-priced services enter the final amount you charged.
6. **Cash jobs:** collect the cash on site. The job is recorded as paid when you complete it.
7. **History** lists finished jobs. **Earnings** shows your commission. **Profile** lets you switch Available for new jobs on or off.

## Administrators

**Every day**
1. Open Overview. Awaiting action shows bookings that need you.
2. In Bookings, filter by Pending and press Approve.
3. For Confirmed bookings press Assign and choose a technician. If the message says the technician already has a job in that slot, choose someone else.
4. If a technician rejects a job, it returns to Confirmed. Assign it again.
5. To cancel a booking, press Cancel and give a reason. Administrator cancellations carry no fee.

**Money**
- **Payments** lists online and cash payments. Filter by Refunds due and press Refund. The customer receives the paid amount minus any cancellation fee. Each refund can only be issued once.

**Catalog and people**
- **Categories and Services:** add, edit, disable or delete. Disable a service instead of deleting it once it has bookings.
- **Technicians:** add a technician with a temporary password, set skills and commission, mark them unavailable (for example on leave) or deactivate them. You cannot deactivate a technician who has active jobs; reassign those first.
- **Customers:** search, and deactivate an account if needed.

**Quality and records**
- **Reviews:** hide a review that breaks your rules. Hidden reviews disappear from the public site.
- **Messages:** contact-form messages from the website.
- **Activity log:** every important action, who did it and when, including security events such as account lockouts.

**Settings**
- Time slots, how many bookings each slot allows, how far ahead customers can book, minimum notice, cash on service on or off, and the cancellation policy. Changes apply to new bookings straight away.

## Common questions

**A customer says a time slot is missing.** The slot is full, already in the past, or inside the minimum-notice window. Check Settings, and raise the bookings-per-slot number if you have more technicians.

**A customer cannot pay online for an estimate service.** That is by design: payment opens once the technician has set the final amount.

**An account is locked.** After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 15 minutes. It unlocks by itself.

**A customer forgot their password.** The system does not have a forgot-password feature yet (see the requirements document). This is the most useful feature to add next.

**Online payment shows as not available.** The Razorpay keys are missing on the server, or the placeholder values are still in place.

**Emails do not arrive.** Check the mail settings on the server, and that the sender address is verified with your email provider. Check spam folders too.
